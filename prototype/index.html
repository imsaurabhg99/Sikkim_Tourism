TOURISM SERVICE

Sathi — your local companion

Verified & trained

"Sathi" means companion. A local Sikkimese host — identity-verified and trained in hospitality and first response — who plans with you, books for you, and makes you feel less like a tourist and more like a friend visiting. Pick only the parts you want — each is priced separately in the estimator.

PLAN & BOOK

Itinerary planning & bookings

Your Sathi shapes the itinerary around your pace, then books the tickets, cars and rooms — before you even land.

MOST POPULAR DURING YOUR TRIP

On-trip assistance

A local friend on call every day — hidden food gems, the monastery worth the detour, help in any emergency, and no language barrier: your Sathi speaks the local languages and English for you.

ARRIVAL & DEPARTURE

Pickup & drop

Met at the airport or railhead and dropped back safely — especially reassuring for solo and first-time international travellers.

VISITING FROM OUTSIDE INDIA?

Permits, handled.

Foreign nationals need an Inner Line Permit for Sikkim and extra permits for protected areas like Tsomgo and the trekking routes. We tell you exactly what applies to your itinerary and arrange the paperwork with your operator.
